About Us – Urbahn Architects

Urbahn Architects is a New York-based architectural practice with offices throughout the region, dedicated to shaping resilient, people-first public environments. For nearly eight decades, our team has partnered with civic, educational, transportation, and healthcare leaders to create transformative spaces that serve communities across the globe.

View Our History

Founded in 1945, Urbahn Architects has grown from a local New York City studio into an award-winning firm operating across the United States and internationally. Our portfolio spans more than 40 million square feet of built work, from state-of-the-art university campuses and transit hubs to mission-critical healthcare facilities and community-focused civic spaces.

Our practice embraces an inclusive, research-driven process that connects design excellence with operational performance. We prioritize equity, sustainability, and long-term value, ensuring that every project is rooted in the aspirations of the people it serves.

Shaping Public Realms

We design vibrant, accessible public spaces that encourage interaction and support the civic mission of the institutions we serve. Our approach blends contextual sensitivity with bold gestures that elevate the human experience.

Architecture as a Catalyst

Each project is an opportunity to catalyze positive change. By integrating resilient systems, equitable design strategies, and dynamic programming, we create environments that empower communities well into the future.

Collaborative Design Process

Our interdisciplinary team partners closely with clients, stakeholders, and consultants. Through transparent communication, iterative prototyping, and immersive visualization, we deliver solutions tailored to complex program needs.
